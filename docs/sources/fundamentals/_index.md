# Introduction

This section introduces observability in general and Grafana in particular. Grafana is the open and composable observability platform that gives people and AI agents the context they need to monitor, understand, and act on their data. These topics help you if you're just starting out with observability and monitoring, and explain the different ways you can run Grafana.
